      How to play

      1. On your turn, tap an empty cell or one of yours to add an orb.
      2. Each cell explodes when it holds as many orbs as it has neighbours:
        233234432332
      3. An explosion throws one orb into each neighbour and captures it — all its orbs become your colour.
      4. Captured cells can explode too, setting off a chain reaction.
      5. Lose all your orbs and you're out. Last player standing wins.

      Tip: shaking orbs are one away from exploding. Don't build next to an opponent's shaking cell!
