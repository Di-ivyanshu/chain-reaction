## Full description (max 4000)
Atom Burst is the classic Chain Reaction strategy game, rebuilt to be fast, smooth and made for playing with friends.

💥 HOW IT WORKS
Tap a cell to add an atom. When a cell gets too full it bursts, sends atoms to its neighbours and captures them. One move can set off a huge chain reaction across the whole board. Wipe out every other colour to win!

🌐 PLAY ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
• Create a room and share the code or link (one tap on WhatsApp)
• 2 to 8 players, no account and no sign-up
• Works on Wi-Fi and mobile data
• If the host leaves, another player takes over and the game continues

💬 CHAT & REACTIONS
• Text chat and one-tap quick chat: GG, LG!!, WP and more
• Spoken voice lines like "Let's go!!" and "Revenge time!"
• Push-to-talk voice chat (optional)
• Emoji reactions that pop up on the board

🎮 MORE WAYS TO PLAY
• Local game: pass-and-play on one phone
• vs Computer: Easy, Normal and Hard bots (mix bots and humans too)
• 4 board sizes: Small, Classic, Large and Huge
• Optional turn timer

✨ POLISHED & FUN
• Smooth animations and satisfying explosions
• Neon, Matte Dark and Matte Light themes
• Pick your colour and avatar
• Haptic feedback, optional undo, rematch voting and shareable result cards

🛡️ SAFE & PRIVATE
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts
• Mute or report any player
• Local and vs Computer modes work offline
